The Best HBURG Coffee Shops

Harrisonburg has plenty of great coffee shops, but Broad Porch, Black Sheep, Drifters, and Greenberry’s stand out for their unique atmosphere, quality coffee, and local feel. Whether you’re looking for a cozy place to study, a quick morning coffee, or a spot to meet up with friends, these four cafés offer something for everyone. Together, they showcase some of the best local coffee experiences Harrisonburg has to offer.

Location & Discription

Broad Porch

47 Court Square, Harrisonburg, VA 22801

A warm and welcoming downtown café known for freshly brewed coffee, espresso drinks, tea, and a food menu. Broad Porch also roasts its own coffee and offers a comfortable space to relax, study, or meet up with friends.

Black Sheep

80 E Market St, Harrisonburg, VA 22801

A cozy coffee shop just off Court Square, Black Sheep specializes in espresso, drip coffee, and pour-over drinks. They also offer loose-leaf tea, specialty beverages, and baked goods made in-house.

Drifter's Cafe

217 S Liberty St, Suite 104, Harrisonburg, VA 22801

A local café in downtown Harrisonburg serving coffee alongside breakfast, lunch, sweets, and other café favorites. Drifters has a relaxed, community-focused atmosphere and offers both indoor and outdoor seating.

Greenberry's Coffee Co.

400 S High St, Harrisonburg, VA 22801

A longtime Virginia coffee company serving small-batch, craft-roasted coffee. The Harrisonburg café offers coffee, cold brew, breakfast and lunch options, sandwiches, and baked goods in a comfortable community setting.
